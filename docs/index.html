How it works

  1. Choose products and preview the prices

    Pick the products and the discount: a percent or an amount off, taken from the current price or from the compare-at price, with a price ending such as .99. The preview shows the new prices and flags any that fall below cost (when Shopify has the cost), price endings that move the discount by more than 5 points, and crossed-out prices above the EU 30-day lowest price.

  2. Schedule it, or test it on one product first

    Set the start and end in your store's time zone, or start the sale yourself. To be sure, run it on a single product first: SafeRevert sets the sale price, reads it back from Shopify, restores the original and reads that back too. When the sale starts, SafeRevert saves each variant's price and compare-at price before changing anything.

  3. When it ends, your prices come back

    At the end time, or when you end the sale yourself, SafeRevert puts back each saved price and compare-at price, including an empty compare-at price. If you changed a product's price during the sale, SafeRevert leaves that product as you set it.

    Afterwards it reads the prices back from Shopify and shows the result for each product.

What happens if…

…I change a price myself during the sale?

Your price stays. SafeRevert notices that the price was changed outside the app and stops managing that product, so ending the sale doesn't overwrite your change. It leaves that product's compare-at price as it is too: if you changed only the price, check whether you still want the crossed-out price.

When the sale ends, SafeRevert shows how many products kept your price.

…two or three sales include the same product?

Shoppers see the lowest of the sale prices; the discounts don't add up. When one of the sales ends, the product moves to the lowest price among the sales still running, and its original price and compare-at price come back when the last of them ends.

…SafeRevert is down when a sale should end?

SafeRevert can't change anything in your store while it's down, so the sale prices stay live until it's back. Then it restores every overdue sale first, before it starts any new sale. A sale that should have started in the meantime starts then, unless its end time has already passed.

…I uninstall SafeRevert during a sale?

Shopify removes an app's access to your store the moment it's uninstalled. After that SafeRevert can no longer change your prices, so the sale prices stay live. Before you uninstall, use Restore all prices in SafeRevert: it ends every running sale and puts the original prices back while it still has access.

If you uninstall during a sale anyway and reinstall within 48 hours, SafeRevert still has your original prices and offers to restore them.

…another app or a price sync changes prices during the sale?

SafeRevert treats it like a manual edit: those products keep the price the other app set, and ending the sale doesn't change them back. If an ERP, POS or feed app syncs prices into Shopify, pause that sync while a sale runs.

The receipt after the sale lists each of these products.

…I sell in other countries with fixed market prices?

SafeRevert changes each product's base price and compare-at price. Shopify's help center notes that fixed prices you set for a market override the prices and compare-at prices set in your Shopify admin, so the sale won't show in a market where a product has a fixed price. Read Shopify's article on sale prices.

…my store sells in the EU (30-day lowest price rule)?

SafeRevert keeps a record of each product's price from the day you install it. The preview warns when a crossed-out price would be above the lowest price of the last 30 days, and you can have SafeRevert cap the crossed-out price at that lowest price during the sale; your original compare-at price comes back when the sale ends.

Because the record starts at install, it covers fewer than 30 days at first, and the preview shows how many days it covers. SafeRevert is a tool, not legal advice.

…a product is deleted during the sale?

SafeRevert skips it and still restores every other product. The receipt lists it as deleted in Shopify.

…a sale has more variants than my plan allows?

The sale doesn't start, and SafeRevert never upgrades your plan or adds charges on its own. Upgrade, or make a smaller sale.

The preview tells you whether a sale fits your plan before you schedule it.

…I need to stop a sale early?

End it at any time and its original prices come back, with your own edits kept. If a large sale is still being applied when you stop it, SafeRevert stops before the next batch of products and restores the ones it already changed.

…I want to change or cancel a scheduled sale?

You can cancel a sale before it starts, change when a running sale ends, or duplicate a past sale to run it again. A running sale's discount and products stay as you previewed them; to change those, end it and start a new sale.

Other questions

Can I try a sale on one product first?

Yes. The one-product test sets the sale price on a single product, reads it back from Shopify, restores the original and reads that back too. The test price is kept out of the EU 30-day price record.

Which store currencies work?

Stores whose Shopify currency is one of these 19: USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, NZD, CHF, SEK, NOK, DKK, PLN, CZK, RON, RSD, BAM, SGD, HKD, MXN and BRL. For now SafeRevert can't run sales in other currencies.

Does SafeRevert see my customers or orders?

No. SafeRevert asks Shopify only for permission to read and edit products, so it can't see orders or customers, and it stores no customer data.

Why not Shopify Rollouts or automatic discounts?

Both are built into Shopify and worth knowing. They do a different job.

Automatic discounts

They're applied in the cart and at checkout. Shopify's help center says that showing discount savings as crossed-out prices on product and collection pages needs a third-party discount app.

Rollouts

Available on the Basic plan and up, and since October 23, 2026 able to schedule market catalogs. A rollout switches a catalog on or off: it changes only the catalog's status. By default a catalog's percentage adjustment lowers compare-at prices too, edits to the catalog's prices during a rollout are saved right away, and Shopify doesn't restore a copy of the catalog from when the rollout started.

SafeRevert

Changes each product's own price and compare-at price after saving the original of every variant in the sale, and puts those originals back when the sale ends, except where you changed a price yourself.

Sources: Shopify Help Center articles on sale prices, Rollouts requirements and catalogs, and the Shopify changelog of October 23, 2026.

    • the app restarting in the middle of a sale
